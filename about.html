Who We Are

Delivering Reliable Logistics Solutions with Professional Excellence.

NEX Logistics International Freight Solutions is a trusted logistics and transportation company dedicated to providing reliable freight solutions for businesses across Saudi Arabia, the GCC, and international markets. We combine industry expertise with efficient logistics planning to ensure every shipment is handled safely, professionally, and on schedule.

Our comprehensive services include road transportation, air freight, ocean freight, freight forwarding, container transportation, project cargo handling, and supply chain support. Every solution is tailored to meet our customers' operational requirements while maintaining the highest standards of safety, reliability, and service quality.
